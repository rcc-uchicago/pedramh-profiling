# Addendum 1 (2026-11-02, before any result of this chain exists): the "extend G4D past e63?" rule

Operator: "since we are interested in 5 year rollouts, shouldn't the accuracy over a longer period be
optimized more?" Agreed. The decision to train G4D further is therefore taken on **Part 2 (5-yr protocol)**,
not on K=56. Part 1 stays descriptive.

**EXTEND** (another 60 epochs, to e123 = a cycle end) only if all three hold:
1. **Climate improves with training at matched cycle position.** G4D56 − G4D16 (paired dA) < −3.3 SE.
   **Or** G4D36 → G4D56 is still falling, with G4D56 − G4D36 < 0 by more than 1 SE.
2. **Stability holds late.** G4D43, G4D56 and G4D63 all pass gates 1–3 (8/8 healthy, water, |dPS| ≤ 1 hPa).
   There must be no G1-dry-style late collapse.
3. **Drift does not grow.** The drifting groups of G4D56/G4D63 are ≤ 3 (gate 4).

**STOP** otherwise. The ~92 node-h then goes to a second G4D seed.

The K=56 rows (6 h / 24 h) are reported but do not enter this decision.
